How an invoice moves through AvixCheck

Every step is tracked, and nothing uncertain is passed silently.

  1. Import

    Suppliers, purchase orders, price agreements, approved charges and receipts from CSV templates, with a preview and row-level errors.

  2. Upload & scan

    PDF invoices go to private storage and are virus-scanned first. Infected files are quarantined.

  3. Extract

    AI reads the header and line items. Low-confidence or incomplete results go to manual correction.

  4. Match

    Supplier, PO, PO lines and the agreement in force on the invoice date. Weak matches go to review.

  5. Check

    Fixed rules compare expected and actual values and record exactly which PO, agreement and settings were used.

  6. Review & export

    Assign exceptions, record decisions, accept authorised variances with reasons, export to CSV.

What it checks

Financial checks follow fixed rules with explicit rounding and tolerances you set. AI is used only to read documents. Each discrepancy amount is potential exposure, kept separate from confirmed corrections.

CheckFlags when
Price varianceUnit price is above the dated agreement price (or PO price) beyond tolerance.
Missing discountAn agreement discount applies at the billed quantity but was not given.
Excess quantityQuantity exceeds the PO line's remaining balance.
Above received quantityQuantity exceeds what was received, checked only when receipt data exists.
PO balance exceededCumulative billing across invoices goes above the PO value.
Arithmetic errorQuantity × price, line totals, subtotal, tax or total do not add up.
Possible duplicateSame document, same supplier and invoice number, or same supplier, total and date.
Unapproved chargeA freight, handling or other charge has no approved charge, or exceeds its limit.
Currency or unit mismatchInvoice currency or unit differs from the PO, agreement or supplier.
Missing or conflicting referenceSupplier, PO, PO line or agreement is missing, ambiguous, closed or cancelled.
Missing dataRequired invoice or line fields are missing.

Credit notes restore PO balance, partial invoices bill across PO lines, and amended POs are versioned. A PO balance is never counted twice.

Security and data

Invoices come from suppliers, so their contents are treated as untrusted throughout.

  • Organisation isolation so no company can see another's suppliers, invoices or files.
  • Private documents opened only through short-lived links after a permission check.
  • Virus scanning before any processing, with upload size limits and PDF validation.
  • Secure sign-in with multi-factor authentication.
  • Audit log of imports, edits, checks, assignments and decisions.
  • Retention controls with legal hold, and verified backups.
